Introduction

At PDFImagera, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our free online PDF tools. We believe in complete transparency and data protection.

Key Principle: All processing happens entirely in your browser (client-side). Your files are never uploaded to our servers.

Data We Collect

We do NOT collect or store any personal data or files.

When you use PDFImagera:

  • Files: Your PDFs, images, and documents are processed entirely in your browser. They are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any server.
  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or any other personal information.
  • Analytics: We do not use tracking cookies or analytics tools that collect personal data.
  • Local Storage: Some settings may be stored locally in your browser for convenience (e.g., language preferences). This data never leaves your device.

How We Process Data

All processing is done client-side using WebAssembly and JavaScript libraries loaded directly in your browser:

  • PDF.js: For PDF rendering and text extraction
  • jsPDF: For PDF generation from images and text
  • pdf-lib: For PDF merging, splitting, and organization
  • XLSX: For Excel file parsing

Your files are processed locally and never leave your device. When you download a result, it's generated entirely in your browser.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may load in your browser:

  • Tailwind CSS: For styling (loaded from CDN)
  • Font Awesome: For icons (loaded from CDN)
  • Google Fonts: For typography (loaded from CDN)
  • PDF Libraries: PDF.js, jsPDF, pdf-lib, XLSX (loaded from CDN)

These services are loaded as resources only. They do not have access to your files or personal data.

Some CDN providers may log anonymous requests. We do not have control over their logging policies.

Cookies

PDFImagera uses minimal cookies or local storage only for:

  • User Preferences: Remembering your selected tool or settings (optional)
  • Session Management: No session cookies are used

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies. You can use PDFImagera with cookies disabled.

Data Security

Since no data is ever transmitted to our servers, the security of your data is determined by:

  • Your Browser: Modern browsers implement strong security measures
  • HTTPS: All connections use HTTPS encryption
  • Local Processing: Your files remain in your device's memory only during processing

We never have access to your files. They are never stored, cached, or transmitted.

Children's Privacy

PDFImagera is a general audience service. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. Since we don't collect any data, no special provisions are needed.

Your Rights

Under GDPR and other privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of any data we might hold (none)
  • Deletion: Request deletion of any data (none stored)
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data (none stored)
  • Portability: Transfer your data (none stored)

Since we don't store any personal data, these rights are automatically respected.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
